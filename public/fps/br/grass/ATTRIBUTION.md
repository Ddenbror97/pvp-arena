# Ground grass (Project Island)

All CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). `grass-v1.webp` (atlas) and
`grass-v1.bin` (geometry) are derived from the clumps below by `art/br/grass/build_grass.py`:
chosen clumps only, recentred on their foot, textures cropped, rescaled to one texel density,
packed into one atlas and their colour bled into the transparent pixels.
