- `sedan`: "Murphy '92 - Low poly model" by Daniel Zhabotinsky (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/murphy-92-low-poly-model-bae7fbb58bc04de1ab2d62cd056a4652), licensed under CC BY 4.0. Reoriented, rescaled, simplified and re-textured; wheels separated for steering and rolling.
- `suv`: "Bokaroo '67 - Low poly model" by Daniel Zhabotinsky (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/bokaroo-67-low-poly-model-22015d1863d6455aa31cfd738b972c50), licensed under CC BY 4.0. Reoriented, rescaled, simplified and re-textured; wheels separated for steering and rolling.
- `pickup`: "Rancher '80 Lifted - Low Poly model" by Daniel Zhabotinsky (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/rancher-80-lifted-low-poly-model-fbf1767c4c3a450caa59c6431af79263), licensed under CC BY 4.0. Reoriented, rescaled, simplified and re-textured; wheels separated for steering and rolling.
- `bike`: "DIRT BIKE OFF ROAD BIKE LOW POLY" by nabeelashrafphotography (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/dirt-bike-off-road-bike-low-poly-dbcca404b21348c69938207d265b6035), licensed under CC BY 4.0. Reoriented, rescaled, simplified and re-textured; wheels separated for steering and rolling.

## The flyable aircraft (plane-c172.lod{0,1,2}.glb)

Built by art/blender/br/build_plane.mjs:

- `plane-c172`: "FREE Cessna 172SP" by NLM (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/free-cessna-172sp-c9cadc2f026946da8cf9715a683739e9), licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Reoriented and rescaled to real size, simplified and re-textured; the "remove before flight" ribbons and tie-down blocks removed; the propeller, ailerons, flaps, elevator, rudder and wheels separated on their hinges; the registration on the right side un-mirrored.
