# Unsourced guides: audit and the first fix

224 published guides had an empty `sources` array after the 21 October 2026 quality release. This pass did not rewrite them. It checked whether they state a third-party RTP, a licence, or a bonus term that a reader could mistake for a measured fact.

Most of the percentages in these files are this site's own fees (5% of a coinflip or jackpot pot, about 7.88% on roulette after the fee, 10% of a P2P Slott win, which is also written as the winner keeping 90%). Reviews and slot explainers generally leave the brand's RTP, max win, and licence number as "not published". A scan for affirmative patterns found no "RTP is {number}", no "holds an MGA/Curacao licence", no "we tested", and no "20x wagering" stated as a live offer.

Eighteen files contain some other percentage. Sixteen of those are classroom arithmetic (a -130 price breaking even near 56.5%), a suppose ("do not write pays 96%"), a cross-link to a different title whose own page already cites the studio, or the 90% left after this site's 10% Slott fee. They were left as written.

## What changed

Three craps pages stated a house edge with no source. Wikipedia's craps article records the same figures, so those pages now cite it:

- `craps-field-bet`: 5.56% when 2 and 12 both pay 2 to 1, and 2.78% when 12 pays 3 to 1.
- `crapless-craps` and the one sentence in `online-craps`: 5.38% house edge on the crapless variant.

A felt that pays a different schedule is still "not published" on these pages. No RTP, licence number, or bonus term was added for any casino or slot.

## What this pass did not do

The other 221 unsourced guides were not given a citation. Attaching this site's terms to every page that mentions a 5% fee would clear the warning without adding a new fact, and it was not done here. Brand RTPs and licence statuses stay blank until someone opens the studio page or the regulator's register and cites that page.
