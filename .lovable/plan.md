# 30-guide SEO roadmap for PVPspinArena (research only, no pages built)

This produces a research document, not website changes. Guides are written only after you approve the roadmap.

## Research steps
1. **Keyword data from Semrush (US market by default, since the site is in English with a global audience):**
   - Compare about 80–100 candidate keywords on search volume, keyword difficulty, CPC and intent, across the topics in your brief: crypto casino, Bitcoin/ETH/USDT/USDC casino, provably fair, crypto roulette/coinflip/jackpot, deposits, withdrawals, wallets, security, Web3 casino, terminology.
   - Deep dives on the roughly 15 strongest ones: related terms, long-tail variants and question queries.
   - SERP checks (who ranks, how hard it is) for about 10 key terms.
2. **Competitors:** use Semrush to find organic competitors and keyword gaps for pvpspinarena.com and 2–3 known crypto-casino content sites. The site is new, so its own data will likely be close to empty. I'll say so rather than guess.
3. **Content-gap check:** a light look at top-ranking pages through web search, to see what they cover and what PVPspinArena can add (real PvP mechanics, verifiable fairness, the on-site verifier, USDC on Base).
4. **Existing site pages** used as linking targets: Jackpot, Coinflip, Roulette, Fairness, How it works, About, Responsible gambling and Wallet.

## Deliverable
A single file, `/mnt/documents/pvpspinarena-seo-roadmap.md`, with the 7 sections your brief asks for:
1. Research summary: opportunities, competitor patterns, gaps, clusters, intent, risks.
2. A 30-guide master table: primary keyword, MSV, KD, CPC, intent, cluster, slug, meta title, meta description, H1, FAQ questions, target site page, priority.
3. Topical clusters (6–10 of them).
4. Internal linking map: guide to guide, and guide to site page.
5. 5–10 quick wins.
6. Publishing order, pillar pages first so topical authority builds up.
7. A Semrush verification list for every keyword where data was missing.

## Rules applied
- Every number comes from a Semrush tool call. Missing data is marked "Semrush data unavailable — requires manual Semrush verification". Nothing is invented.
- Titles are 40–59 characters and descriptions 120–159, checked by a script. Slugs and primary keywords are unique, with a cannibalisation check.
- No claims of licensing or legality, no promises of profit, no "risk-free" wording.

## Technical notes
- Semrush numbers are estimates of Google searches, and I'll label them that way.
- Gambling keywords are country-sensitive. US data may understate demand elsewhere, so I'll state which market was used and can add UK or other markets on request.
