## Blackwater Prison on a rocky knoll

The knoll is generated by `build_world.py` (`PRISON_KNOLL`, `prison_knoll`).

### Shape

- **The top:** the yard is now level at 85 m, 21 m higher than before.
- **The edge:** the top runs 163-206 m out from the centre, with a wobbled outline. It is pulled in on the east, where the country road passes 215 m out, so the road keeps a smooth cutting there instead of leaving a 20 m step.
- **The rock face:** below the top edge is a 13 m face at a 0.85 slope, which a player can walk up. Ledges of 1-2.5 m break it up so it reads as rock, not a smooth bank. The land cover is bare rock all the way round.
- **The skirt:** below the face, a 0.5 slope runs down to the surrounding ground.
- **The south-west:** the higher ground there is lowered towards the foot of the face, so the face shows on every side.
- **The north side:** the face drops into the river valley, about 55 m below the top. It is the prison's most dramatic side.

### Access

- **South gate:** the prison now has a road, on a 10 % ramp up the face. It is added after the road network is joined up, so the network does not treat its end inside the yard as a loose end. It meets the road south of the prison.
- **North gate:** no road can climb the north face, so this gate opens onto the rocks, which players can climb.

### Rocks

25 boulders and outcrops sit in the face all the way round. They give cover on the climb and are tagged `nature`, which places them on the terrain rather than on the pad.

## The prison inside the walls

610 placements, up from 20 (`layout.ts prison`, `prisonInterior`):

- **Three cell houses** round the exercise yard, built from the downloaded brick cell block (`prison-block`). They are enterable (see the next section).
- **The exercise yard:** two basketball courts, bleachers, two shelters with picnic tables, and a chain-link fence round it. Floodlight masts stand at the yard's corners.
- **The dead zone:** a chain-link fence 9 m inside the wall all the way round, leaving the gates open. Floodlights stand at its corners.
- **Towers:** eight guard towers, one in each bastion and a pair inside each gate.
- **South gate:**
  - the intake block (the police station, with cells and an armoury);
  - the sally port, with two transport buses and barriers;
  - the laundry and the commissary;
  - the visitors' car park outside the gate.
- **Barracks:** four dormitory barracks.

## The cell house, enterable

The fit-out is `art/br/fitout/prison.py`, written to `building-access-v10`.

### The building

- **Size:** 52 x 12.7 m.
- **Wings:** two four-storey wings, with their roofs at 12 m.
- **Centre:** a lower centre section with its front set back and its roof at 9.8 m.

### Inside the wings

- **Cells:** each wing is a cell house with two rows of four cells, on three tiers (floors at 0.06, 4.9 and 9.82 m).
- **Galleries:** they run along the cell fronts, with an open well down the middle and rails on the open edges.
- **Stairs:** a switchback stair in each well. The first flight rises from the ground to a landing at the wing's end. The second turns back and rises to the top tier.

### The roof terrace

The top tier is level with the centre's roof. A door from each wing leads out onto it: a terrace between the wings, overlooking the yard.

### The centre

- **Guard hall:** on the ground floor, with the guard desk and the main and back doors.
- **Control floor:** above the guard hall. It joins both wings' first tiers.

### Doors and cells

- **Ways in:** four doors on the ground (main, back, and one at each wing's end), plus the two roof doors. Six doors in all, drawn in `doors.v8`.
- **Barred cells (the riot's aftermath):** half the cells are barred shut, alternating along each row and tier. The bars are a placement (`cell-bars`). Each barred cell is also solid in the block's own collision, so no loot spawns in a cell nobody can enter.
- **Open cells:** the other half stand open. Every cell has a bunk (`prison-bed`).
- **Loot:** 24 military-class items per block, like the police station's armoury.

### Walk tests

`building-access-routes.json` walks every way in, plus the full climb in each wing: in at the end door, up both flights and out onto the roof terrace. It does this at all four turns and at the real placements.

## Riverwood

### Relief

The town grid itself stays flat, because its buildings sit at the pad's height.

- **Blend:** the flat blend around the grid is cut from 220 m to 120 m (`TOWNS["riverwood"]["blend"]`).
- **Three new hills** (not ridged, so the shared noise is unchanged):
  - a wooded hill to the south-west (+34 m);
  - a rise west of the town (+22 m);
  - a bluff across the main river to the north (+30 m).

### The waterfront

`layout.ts riverwoodWaterfront`, on the north bank between the town and the main river:

- **The lumber mill:**
  - two sawmill sheds, the mill warehouse and a Quonset hut;
  - the site office;
  - twelve log stacks, plank stacks and a row of logs;
  - the log loader (an excavator), forklifts and trucks;
  - lot lights.
- **The lumber yard's ground:** bare earth, so no trees grow between the stacks (`RIVERWOOD_YARD`).
- **The river:** four jetties with motorboats and rowboats, and a scrap-wood shack up the bank behind each.
