## Custom-made for this map

- Building and facade kit (build_sandgate.py): every 8 m block dressed from the layout grid — inset plaster / sandstone / adobe / concrete-and-cladding walls, flush plinths, string courses, cornices, quoins and pilasters, recessed windows with stone surrounds and sills, open and closed shutters, iron grilles, doors, recessed loggias with railings, AC and cabinet niches, drainpipes in chases, shop boards; upper storeys, parapets and terracotta lean-to roofs above the 8 m collision.
- Covered passages (Police passages, B hall, lower passage): plaster over ceramic-tile wainscot, timber-beamed ceilings, lamps; the Police guard office roof and ceiling.
- Gameplay cover built to its box: stairs, A platform, A catwalk, B loading dock with hazard edge and bumpers, gate piers with recessed timber leaves and beams, guard booth, B well, B planters, pillars, spawn and corridor walls, office walls, cast-concrete blast-wall segments (A long), vehicle underbody fills, forklift pallet.
- Ground: per-area asphalt, concrete slabs, flagstones, yard concrete and dirt with vertex-colour variation; decals for sand drifts, cracks, oil, tyre tracks, drains, manholes, road paint, plant-zone outlines and the painted A / B letters and arrows (plain letters, no branding).
- Skyline: exterior ring buildings, water towers, rooftop tanks / AC boxes / masts, overhead cables, horizon hills.
- Procedural textures (textures.py): terracotta barrel-tile roofs, painted wood, painted corrugated metal, the facade trim atlas (window, shutter, doors, roller shutter, grille, vent, AC front, panel, cabinet, sign, beam, recess, ceramic tiles, hazard stripes, soil) and the decal atlas.
