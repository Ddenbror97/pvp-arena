## Spec 1 — Circle rebalance and the M tactical map

### A. Audit findings (before)

- 9 phases, **32.5 min** from take-off; gas 0.4 → 11 HP/s. Early gas (0.4 HP/s) was so weak that
  players ignored it for most of the match.
- Circle centres could move nearly the full `R − r`: some rotations could not be run at a sprint.
- Late circles could land in water, on cliffs, across a river far from a bridge, or inside a building.
- The client drew the circle only at snapshot times, so the circle edge stepped instead of moving smoothly.
- There was no full-screen map. The minimap only showed the area around the player.
- When several players died to the gas in the same tick they got the same place (two "#1"s), and a
  match could end with no winner.

### B. Changes

- `zone.ts` rewritten: there are 3 presets picked by seat count (≥60 full, ≥30 medium, otherwise small).
  Radii are shares of a 5 km reference radius. Gas exposure ramps up the damage.
- **Placement uses the map.** Each candidate centre must pass these checks:
  - enough dry, walkable ground inside it;
  - no river trap without a bridge nearby;
  - from phase 5 on, the centre must be open ground; the room checks this with a "not inside a building" test.
- The centre moves at most `0.5 × (R − r)` (`ZONE_SHIFT`), so every rotation fits at a sprint.
- **Gas exposure:** damage rises by up to +50 % over 30 s outside, and decays twice as fast once you are back inside.
  The view sends each player their effective HP/s.
- The client interpolates the circle on the server clock between snapshots.
- **Tactical map on M**, drawn from the game's own data, so it matches the world by construction:
  - terrain: land cover, hill shading, 20 m contours, water by depth;
  - rivers, roads, bridges and the dam, runways;
  - every building footprint, place names, the 1 km grid A–J / 1–10.
- Live layers on the map:
  - gas, the current circle (white), the next circle (yellow, dashed), the line to the next zone;
  - you with your facing, the plane path, supply drops, the waypoint.
- Map controls: wheel zoom, drag, click to set a waypoint (it shows as a light beam in the world), right-click to clear,
  R to recentre, F to fit.
- **Results:** players eliminated in the same tick are ranked by kills, then damage, then seat. Every match has exactly one winner.

### C. Final circle configuration (full preset, 100 seats)

| Phase | Wait (s) | Shrink (s) | End radius | Gas HP/s |
| ----- | -------- | ---------- | ---------- | -------- |
| 1     | 300      | 150        | 3 250 m    | 1        |
| 2     | 90       | 125        | 2 250 m    | 2        |
| 3     | 80       | 95         | 1 450 m    | 3        |
| 4     | 60       | 75         | 900 m      | 5        |
| 5     | 50       | 60         | 500 m      | 7        |
| 6     | 40       | 50         | 250 m      | 10       |
| 7     | 30       | 45         | 100 m      | 14       |
| Final | 20       | 60         | 0 m        | 18       |

- The first circle reaches at most 3 900 m from the island centre.
- The match runs **≈ 22 min** from take-off (it was 32.5 min). The medium preset runs ≈ 19 min and the small preset ≈ 15 min (7 phases).
- Gas damage gains up to +50 % over 30 s of exposure.

### D. Tests

These test files pass: `br-zone`, `br-drop`, `br-doors`, `br-static-world`, `br-models`, `br-loot`, `br-vegetation` and `br-world` (8 files, 81 tests).

They cover:

- presets and nesting, and that every rotation fits at a sprint;
- 1 000 seeds of endings: spread out, dry, walkable, no river traps;
- linear shrink, the circle boundary, and exposure against the best heal;
- a full 100-seat match with bots: unique places, and a winner every time.

`br-runtime` (p99 timing) fails only when the machine is under load. It fails the same way on the commit before this session, so it is an environment issue, not a regression.

### E. Verdict: **GO for staging, NO-GO for production until it is playtested**

The logic is deterministic and covered by tests. The pacing (22 min, damage values) has only been tested with bots.
Real players need to judge whether phases 1 and 2 feel too long or too short.

## Spec 2 — In-game UI/UX modernization

### A. Audit (before)

- Large opaque panels covered too much of the world.
- The kill feed overlapped the minimap.
- Gas kills read "Fire".
- No compass, no zone state, no warning that you were taking gas damage.
- The inventory was one large modal with a header row, and you could not take items from the ground inside it.
- The minimap colours did not match the new map (current circle blue, next circle white).

### B. Changes

- **Design tokens** (font, translucent background, warning/danger/gas colours, radius, spacing, 120 ms motion) on `.br-game`.
- **Top:**
  - compass strip with the waypoint marker;
  - match bar "ALIVE | ZONE n | clock";
  - zone state: SAFE ZONE MARKED / GAS ADVANCING / OUTSIDE (pulsing) / FINAL CIRCLE;
  - timed notices.
- **Bottom left:** compact health (icon, number /100) and armour bars, with a red edge when HP is low.
- **Bottom centre:** hotbar with the gun names and keycaps.
- **Bottom right:**
  - ammo panel with the weapon icon, mag / reserve and fire mode (AUTO / BOLT);
  - RELOADING (pulsing), and a low-ammo state shown in yellow;
  - a weapon-switch card shown for 1.4 s.
- **Kill feed:** structured rows (killer, weapon icon, victim). Gas and vehicle kills get their own icons. It now sits beside the minimap.
- **Gas:** a breathing orange vignette and a "−X HP/s" alert while you are outside.
- **Minimap:** white current circle, yellow dashed next circle, tinted gas, the waypoint, and an M key badge.
- **Inventory (B / I):** three panes, and the world stays visible around them:
  - Nearby: loot within reach; click or drag it to take it.
  - Backpack: filters and a capacity bar.
  - Equipment: guns with attachments, wear, plates, and an inspect panel.
  - HTML5 drag and drop works in both directions; double-click uses or equips an item.
- **Overlays:** one at a time (inventory, map, tablet), and Esc closes it. The cursor is freed while an overlay is open, but movement goes on, because the match never pauses.
- **Result and elimination cards:** restyled, with a gold "WINNER" and stats (place, kills, damage).

### C. Layout

The four corners plus the top centre are used. The crosshair is a 4 px dot. Prompts sit low in the centre. The inventory adapts below 1100 px wide.

### D. Tests

- `tsc` is clean; `eslint` shows only the existing fast-refresh warning; `prettier` is clean.
- `scripts/br/client-sim/ui-capture.mjs` mounts the **real React HUD over the real client** and the in-page match (headless Chromium, SwiftShader).
  It captures the plane, the map with a waypoint, the parachute, on foot, the inventory with nearby loot, and the gas.

### E. Not done / limits

- No Tab scoreboard. BR shows "alive" in the match bar; a full table needs per-player stats in the view.
- No settings for HUD scale or colourblind palettes.
- The screenshots come from software WebGL at 1600×900. The HUD has not been checked on a real GPU, at 4K or on ultrawide screens.

### F. Verdict: **GO for staging**. Only visual and CSS risk; no gameplay logic was changed.

## Spec 3 — Parachute physics and POV

### A. Audit (before)

The canopy fell almost as fast as it moved: 13 m/s forward and 17.8 m/s down, a ratio of 0.73.

- From 300 m you could glide only about 220 m, and only about 15 % of the island was reachable per route.
- Steering followed the camera, so looking around also turned the canopy.
- The view was third person only.

### B. Changes

- The canopy flies its own heading; the mouse only looks.
- W glides at 13 m/s forward and 3.6 m/s down (3.6 : 1). Neutral is 8 / 4.6. S brakes for a steep final approach at 3 / 6.5.
- A and D turn at up to 55°/s (radius about 13.5 m). Speeds ease in over about 0.8 s, so nothing snaps.
- There is still no way to cut away before landing.
- Bots fly the canopy with the same inputs.
- **First person under the canopy** (V switches to the chase view):
  - gloved hands on the toggles, pulling on the turning side;
  - risers lifted clear of the view;
  - banking into turns, nose up when braking, a knee bend on landing.
- Other players' canopies face the way they drift.

### C. Measurements

| Metric                           | Before             | After                            |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------ | -------------------------------- |
| Canopy forward / down            | 13 / 17.8 m/s      | 13 / 3.6 m/s (W)                 |
| Glide ratio                      | 0.73 : 1           | 3.6 : 1                          |
| Glide from the 300 m auto-deploy | ~220 m, ~17 s      | ~1.05 km, ~80 s                  |
| From the plane's height          | —                  | ~2.8 km                          |
| Island reachable per route       | ~15 %              | ~60 % (the test requires > 45 %) |
| Turn                             | follows the camera | ≤ 55°/s, radius ~13.5 m          |

### D. Tests

`br-drop` covers:

- glide distance, steady speeds, braking, turn rate and smoothness;
- that looking around does not steer, that you cannot cut away, and that the result does not depend on the step size;
- how much of the island is reachable.

The client-sim also takes screenshots of turning and of looking up at the canopy.

### E. Not tested

How the first-person canopy feels on a real GPU at a high frame rate.
Multiplayer: whether other players' canopies look smooth over a real connection. The headless sim reports the prediction error, but there is no latency.

### F. Verdict: **GO for staging**, then playtest the descent time. About 80 s from 300 m may feel long; it can be tuned with `DROP.parachute.glideDown`.
