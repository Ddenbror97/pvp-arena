## Evidence and presentation

Each prepared article targets 2,400–2,800 reader-visible words, six FAQs, two comparison tables, a checklist and two clearly hypothetical fee examples. The existing guide route supplies one H1, direct answer, takeaways, contents, canonical metadata and FAQ JSON-LD. Money-intent pages reuse the cached, privacy-filtered FPS settled-results block; an empty sample remains empty.

Publisher-level attribution is retained. No named CS2-ranked author or first-hand test is invented. A verified author, rank and their actual testing screenshots remain an editorial gap against the supplied plan. Updated dates mean source/code review, not a fabricated playtest. Screenshots from internal gameplay QA are not presented as a named expert's review.

No US state is declared eligible without a verified current legal basis. Cash eligibility remains subject to the current terms and cashier; skill is not treated as a universal legal exemption. Unblocked topics explain permitted access, not filter circumvention. Browser pages distinguish competitor in-game currencies from cash payouts.
