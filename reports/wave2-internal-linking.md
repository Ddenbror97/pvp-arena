## What was added

Each new guide already linked to older explainers and to its topic hub. Those links were kept. Two gaps were filled:

- One link from the closest existing parent page to each new guide. Gamblers Anonymous also got a second link from the hotline page, because the fellowship and the helpline are different products and both pages are where readers look for help.
- One sibling link between new guides that answer adjacent questions. The anchor is a description, not the destination keyword. Gamblers Anonymous has no wave-2 sibling, so it was not given a forced one.

Old-to-old links were not added. The 20 October audit had already inserted 136 contextual links among the older guides. Another pass through those pairs would not give the new guides an inbound path.
